# Laiba Masood - Business Analyst Portfolio
This portfolio showcases my work in Business Analytics, focusing on data analysis, visualization, and data-driven problem solving - using MS Excel, MS Power BI, SPSS, Python, and more.

Ahdus Technology Post Performance Analysis: This project showcases a detailed Social Media Analytics dashboard where I tracked the performance of content across the company's social media business pages. It tracks different metrics such as reach, engagement, views, organic followers, high-performing days, content themes that stand out, and so on. First, I tracked these metrics on Excel, and later I created a data visualization dashboard in Power BI, which clearly highlights the important themes and factors. I have also added a detailed report on the insights gained from these analytics and the learnings we can use going forward, on both Excel and Power BI. The dashboard is made for a Germany-based IT company, Ahdus Technology, where I currently work.

Thesis - A Machine Learning based Approach to Predict Customer Recommendations in the Airline Industry: This is my MS thesis focusing on customer sentiment analysis in the airline industry. Through comprehensive qualitative and quantitative analysis, the research explains patterns in customer feedback, identifies critical factors driving passenger satisfaction, and constructs a predictive model for anticipating future consumer trends. I compiled, cleaned, and analyzed an extensive dataset of online reviews collected from Tripadvisor, using sentiment analysis revealing key service features like seat comfort, cabin service, and value for money as crucial to customer satisfaction. I evaluated advanced machine learning models including Support Vector Machine (SVM), Logistic Regression (LR), and K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), with Logistic Regression emerging as the most accurate in predicting customer recommendations. Lastly, I provided strategic recommendations emphasizing the need for airlines to enhance customer experience, improve operational efficiency, and use technological advancements to better meet customer expectations and address service quality issues. I have also attached the supporting coding (Python) and Machine Learning models file.

Research Paper - Factors Affecting Adoption of Business Analytics Tools in Organizations: This research paper explores the factors important for the adoption of Business Analytics tools (such as Power BI, Tableau, Looker Studio, Google Analytics, etc.) in organizations across Pakistan. The independent variables of the study are availability of Business Analytics tools, training to use these tools effectively, and employee motivation to adopt these tools in organizations, while the dependent variable is adoption of Business Analytics tools. I collected quantitative data using a systematic questionnaire from various public and private sector organizations. The data analysis included Correlation and Regression Analysis conducted on SPSS. The analysis showed that all three independent variables are positively and significantly correlated to the dependent variable, rendering support to the hypotheses of the study. I also presented this Research Paper at the 22nd International Research Conference held in Islamabad in March 2023, and won the "Best Research Paper" Award.
